## Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Discussion

This is the key question for tonight. From the JotForm intake:

> *"What is your current MRR?"*

For a counseling practice, MRR is typically calculated as:
- **Average session fee x sessions per week x 52 weeks / 12 months**
- Or: **Total annual revenue / 12**

**Industry benchmarks for a 3-location, 50+ year practice:**
| Scenario | Annual Revenue | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative (2-3 providers, modest caseload) | $150K-$250K | $12.5K-$20.8K |
| Mid-range (4-6 providers) | $250K-$500K | $20.8K-$41.7K |
| Growth (7-10+ providers) | $500K-$800K+ | $41.7K-$66.7K+ |

**Marketing budget benchmark:** 7-10% of revenue for a stable practice, 12-15% for growth mode.

| If monthly revenue is... | Growth marketing budget (12%) |
|---|---|
| $15K/mo | $1,800/mo |
| $25K/mo | $3,000/mo |
| $40K/mo | $4,800/mo |
| $60K/mo | $7,200/mo |

The proposed $800/mo management + ~$700-$1,000/mo ad spend ($1,500-$1,800 total) fits within a 10-12% budget **if the practice is doing at least $15K/mo.** If they're doing more, we have room to be more aggressive.

**Action item tonight:** Ask Cindy and Shawn their MRR to calibrate the budget.
