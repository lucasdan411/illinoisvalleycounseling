A 3-milestone journey to take your practice from a best-kept secret to the first call people make when they need help.

The Big Picture

Three locations. 4.3 stars on Google. 670+ Facebook followers. Fifteen-plus years of serving this community.

The foundation is strong. But right now, people in LaSalle County who are searching for a therapist right now are not finding you. And the tools you rely on to run the practice are becoming a bottleneck.

Our goal: Get IVCS in front of people actively looking for help, make it dead simple for them to book, and unblock your operations so you can scale.

⚠
Critical Fix
The Medisoft Bottleneck
The Problem

Medisoft is your current practice management system, but it's creating friction in scheduling, intake, and patient communication. When someone wants to become a patient, the process has too many steps and too much manual work. That kills conversions.

Three paths to unblock this:

Quick
Least impact, fastest fix
Landing page with appointment request form or low-friction callback request. Patients fill a simple form, you call them back. No software change needed.
Low Lift
Upgrade
Better workflow, moderate lift
Upgrade Medisoft to v30 for modern features, integrate with Vosita for online scheduling and patient portal.
Medium Lift
Migrate
Best long-term, biggest lift
Migrate from Medisoft to SimplePractice: built-in telehealth, online booking, digital intake forms, automated reminders.
High Lift

Our recommendation: Start with the Quick fix (landing page) to stop losing patients immediately. Explore Medisoft v30 + Vosita integration as the mid-term play. We'll discuss what works best for your team.
