What We Offer

Therapy for Every Season of Life

Individual, couples, family, and adolescent counseling — in person in Ottawa or Mendota, with most major insurance accepted.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one counseling for anxiety, depression, stress, trauma, addiction, and life transitions. A safe, confidential space that's entirely yours.

Couples Counseling

Rebuild trust, improve communication, and reconnect with your partner. For married couples, partners, and anyone trying to make it work.

Family Therapy

Help your family communicate, heal, and move forward together. For parents and kids, blended families, and everyone in between.

Adolescent Counseling

Teens face a different world. Our therapists help adolescents navigate anxiety, school stress, social pressures, and family conflict.

Trauma & PTSD

Evidence-based trauma treatment, including Prolonged Exposure and Cognitive Processing Therapy, to help you heal from what you've been through.

Substance Use Support

Compassionate, judgment-free support for you or someone you love navigating addiction and recovery.
