What We Offer

Therapy for Every Season of Life

Individual, couples, family, child, and adolescent counseling - in person in Ottawa or Mendota, with most major insurance accepted.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one counseling for anxiety, depression, stress, trauma, addiction, and life transitions. A safe, confidential space that's entirely yours.

Couples Counseling

Rebuild trust, improve communication, and reconnect with your partner. For married couples, partners, and anyone trying to make it work.

Family Therapy

Help your family communicate, heal, and move forward together. For parents and kids, blended families, and everyone in between.

Child & Adolescent Counseling

Specialized counseling for children, teens, and adolescents. Helping young people navigate anxiety, behavior challenges, school stress, social pressures, and family conflict.

Trauma & PTSD

Evidence-based trauma treatment, including Prolonged Exposure and Cognitive Processing Therapy, to help you heal from what you've been through.

Tele-Therapy (Video)

Meet with your therapist securely from home via video. Same licensed care, face to face, without the drive.

Grief & Loss Counseling

Compassionate support through loss, whether recent or long ago. Find a way to carry it and keep living.

Veterans & Military Support

Specialized support for the men and women who served, including trauma-informed care for PTSD.

Older Adult Counseling

Support for life transitions, grief, depression, dementia-related challenges, and family caregivers.

Stress Management

Practical tools to manage stress before it manages you. Mindfulness, coping skills, and sustainable habits.

Substance Use & Recovery

Compassionate, judgment-free support for alcohol and substance use. Meet people where they are, and build a path forward.

Types of Therapy We Offer

Evidence-Based Approaches

Read our guide → , a plain-English breakdown of how each type of therapy works and who it helps.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Identify and reshape unhelpful thought patterns to change how you feel and act.

Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)

Build skills in mindfulness, emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and relationships.

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)

Accept what's out of your control and commit to actions that enrich your life.

Play Therapy

Using play to help children express feelings, process experiences, and develop coping skills.

Family Systems Therapy

Understanding individual challenges in the context of family relationships and dynamics.

Solution-Focused Therapy

Focus on your strengths and what's possible rather than dwelling on problems.

Trauma-Focused Therapy

Evidence-based approaches including Prolonged Exposure and CPT for PTSD and trauma recovery.

Interpersonal Psychotherapy

Improve your relationships and social functioning to relieve psychological symptoms.

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing - a structured therapy for processing trauma and distressing memories.
