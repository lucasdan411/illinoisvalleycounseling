What We Offer

Therapy for Every Season of Life

Individual, couples, family, and adolescent counseling - in person in Ottawa or Mendota, with most major insurance accepted.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one counseling for anxiety, depression, stress, trauma, addiction, and life transitions. A safe, confidential space that's entirely yours.

Couples Counseling

Rebuild trust, improve communication, and reconnect with your partner. For married couples, partners, and anyone trying to make it work.

Family Therapy

Help your family communicate, heal, and move forward together. For parents and kids, blended families, and everyone in between.

Adolescent Counseling

Teens face a different world. Our therapists help adolescents navigate anxiety, school stress, social pressures, and family conflict.

Trauma & PTSD

Evidence-based trauma treatment, including Prolonged Exposure and Cognitive Processing Therapy, to help you heal from what you've been through.

Phone Therapy

Talk with your therapist over the phone from anywhere. Same quality care, no commute, no waiting room. Perfect for busy schedules.

Tele-Therapy (Video)

Meet with your therapist securely from home via video. Same licensed care, face to face, without the drive.

Grief & Loss Counseling

Compassionate support through loss, whether recent or long ago. Find a way to carry it and keep living.

Group Therapy

Connect with others facing similar challenges in a supportive, professionally guided group setting.

Veterans & Military Support

Specialized support for the men and women who served, including trauma-informed care for PTSD.

Older Adult Counseling

Support for life transitions, grief, depression, dementia-related challenges, and family caregivers.

Stress Management

Practical tools to manage stress before it manages you. Mindfulness, coping skills, and sustainable habits.

Substance Use & Recovery

Compassionate, judgment-free support for alcohol and substance use. Meet people where they are, and build a path forward.
